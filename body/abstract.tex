\chapter*{Abstract}
\label{chap:abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{\nameref{chap:abstract}}

University curriculum documents --- particularly the Thai Qualifications Framework for Higher Education document (มคอ.2) --- are dense, formal texts written primarily for accreditation bodies rather than for students. Despite containing critical information about program learning outcomes (PLOs), required skills, and career pathways, these documents are rarely read or understood by the students they are intended to benefit.

KUru is a RAG-based web application that transforms the KU มคอ.2 document into an interactive, personalized career advisor. The system uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with Google Gemini API for semantic search and natural-language generation, and a Neo4j knowledge graph for structured PLO--Skill--Career relationship traversal. Students can query the curriculum in natural language with every answer grounded in the official document with page-level citations, explore their PLOs in plain language, visualize their skill gaps on a radar chart, receive career match recommendations with explainable gap analysis, and generate a personalized learning path toward their target career.

The system targets KU students across all year levels and is evaluated against a 50-question advisor-validated test set, targeting chatbot answer accuracy of 80\% or above compared to a BM25 keyword-search baseline.
